Severity guide:
- Critical: application-wide failure or major business flow blocked.
- High: important feature unusable with no reasonable workaround.
- Medium: feature behaves incorrectly but workaround exists.
- Low: minor UI or usability issue.

Priority guide:
- P1: fix immediately.
- P2: fix in the next planned release.
- P3: fix when practical.
